How I handle a tough chat

Example conversation

A late order is the most common complaint in retail support. This example shows the pattern I follow: acknowledge, take ownership, fix it, confirm.

  • 01Acknowledge the frustration in one sentence.
  • 02Say what I am checking and how long it takes.
  • 03Offer a fix, with a backup if it falls through.
  • 04Confirm the outcome and close the loop.
Ticket #48213 · Late deliveryLive chat
CUSTOMERMy order was due Friday and it's Tuesday. This is my mom's birthday gift. I'm really upset.
WENDELYNI'm sorry, that's stressful with a birthday coming up. I'm checking the carrier tracking now and will have an answer in two minutes.

CUSTOMEROkay, that works. Thank you for sorting it out so fast.
